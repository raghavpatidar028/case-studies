Incrementality vs. Attribution

Why Last-Click Attribution Misleads

A customer who was already going to buy sees a retargeting ad right before checkout. Last-click credits the ad. MMM and holdout testing show zero incremental lift. At Infosys, A/B incrementality testing distinguished genuine lift from attribution theater -- improving media budget decisions by 18% and removing spend from channels that looked good on paper but were not driving new revenue.

Test Design

How I Design Experiments

Power analysis to determine required sample size • Randomized control/treatment assignment • Holdout group for true incrementality • Pre-registration of primary metric before launch • Segment-level result breakdowns to catch heterogeneous treatment effects
