02  —  Challenges & My Response

What BofA Needs  ·  What I Bring

Modeling consumer behavior at BofA's scale creates four distinct challenges. Each maps to production work I have already delivered — not for toy datasets, but for 10TB+ enterprise environments with real business stakes.

The Challenge
01
Multivariate Statistical Modeling at Enterprise Scale

Consumer behavior is non-linear, temporal, and multi-signal. Building models that capture purchase patterns, churn propensity, and engagement shifts requires rigorous statistical reasoning — and the infrastructure to run it on tens of millions of customer records.

02
Democratizing Insights with GenAI

Business stakeholders across Consumer, Business, and Wealth Management need behavioral insights but cannot query data warehouses directly. Closing that gap without creating an analyst bottleneck requires GenAI tooling that translates intent into data — accurately and at scale.

03
Production ML Deployment with Model Monitoring

A model that works in development is not a model that works in production. Consumer behavior drifts — seasonally, economically, and behaviorally. Catching that drift before it produces bad decisions requires production monitoring built in from day one.

04
Audit-Ready Documentation in a Regulated Environment

Every model deployed in banking must be explainable, documented, and auditable. Statistical assumptions, feature rationale, validation results, and known limitations must all be traceable — not as an afterthought, but as a production requirement.

My Response
01
XGBoost/ARIMA Ensemble at 95% Accuracy — Infosys Innovation Award

Built a multivariate ensemble combining XGBoost (non-linear behavioral features) and ARIMA (temporal seasonality via STL decomposition) on 10TB+ of consumer data from 12 integrated sources. Applied rigorous OLS and panel data assumption validation throughout. Infosys Innovation Award for highest-impact modeling deliverable.

LangChain/LangGraph GenAI Platform — 40% Faster Insights

Business stakeholders query 10TB+ data in plain English. Served via FastAPI, containerized with Docker. 40% reduction in analyst time-to-insight.

Three-Layer Model Monitoring Framework

Caught two drift events before business impact; response time under 24 hours both times.

04
Model Card Documentation Standard — Adopted Across Teams

Created a Model Card template covering data lineage, feature rationale, validation results, known limitations, monitoring plan, and retraining triggers — stored in Confluence linked to JIRA for full traceability.
