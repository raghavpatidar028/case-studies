The Joint Commission is a leading healthcare accreditation body relied on by thousands of hospitals and health systems. Their BI Developer role demands someone who can bridge complex data models with intuitive executive dashboards -- translating accreditation, compliance, and operational data into trusted, self-service Power BI analytics.

Challenges I Solve
Challenge

Fragmented Reporting Across Business Units

Enterprise teams need consistent, certified semantic models -- not ad hoc spreadsheets that break with every schema change or produce conflicting KPI values.

My Response

Kimball Star Schemas + Certified Semantic Models

Built reusable dimensional models in Oracle and Snowflake using Kimball methodology, improving query performance by 45% and standardizing KPI definitions across 8 business units.

Challenge

Slow, Manual Executive Reporting

Leadership spends hours each month reconciling data from multiple sources into static reports -- missing the real-time visibility needed for strategic decisions.

My Response

Power BI Dashboards with Advanced DAX

Developed Power BI dashboards with custom DAX measures, KPI scorecards, and drill-through pages -- cutting manual reporting by 50+ hours/month and speeding executive decisions by 30%.

Challenge

Data Quality and Governance Gaps

Without row-level security and audit-ready lineage, healthcare organizations face compliance risk when sensitive operational data flows into shared dashboards.

My Response

RLS + Microsoft Purview Data Governance

Implemented Power BI row-level security and Microsoft Purview governance policies ensuring 100% PII compliance, role-based access control, and full lineage tracking across all reporting pipelines.

Challenge

Low BI Adoption Among Non-Technical Users

Powerful dashboards fail if stakeholders can't use them confidently. Self-service analytics only works when users trust the data and know how to navigate the model.

My Response

Semantic Abstraction + User Training Programs

Built logical abstraction layers in OBIEE and Power BI that simplified ad hoc analysis for non-technical users, reducing data request backlog by 22% and cutting new-analyst onboarding time by 25%.
