02  —  Challenges & My Response

What Crocs Needs  ·  What I Bring

Four supply chain analytics challenges drive this role. Each one maps directly to work I delivered for a global CPG client at Infosys — with the same tools and the same operational stakes.

The Challenge
01
Building SC Dashboards That Give Operations Real Visibility

Crocs needs Power BI and Excel dashboards that track supply chain KPIs — OTIF, fill rate, on-time delivery, inventory turnover — in real time across their distribution network. Manual reporting wastes analyst hours and produces stale data by the time decisions are made.

02
Identifying Trends and Anomalies Before They Escalate

Supply chain disruptions — delayed shipments, inventory imbalances, customs holds — often go undetected until they become operational fires. The team needs systematic anomaly detection and trend analysis that flags issues early, not after the fact.

03
Demand Forecasting to Support Replenishment and Planning

Stockouts and excess inventory both hurt. Supporting accurate demand forecasting that aligns production and replenishment with sales trends requires data pipelines, modeling, and integration with operational planning workflows.

04
Data Documentation and Ad Hoc Request Management

Stakeholders across logistics, procurement, and finance rely on the analytics team for ad hoc data pulls and reports. Without clear documentation of data sources and definitions, requests slow down and trust in the data erodes.
