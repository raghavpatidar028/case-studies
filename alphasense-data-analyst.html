02  —  Challenges & My Response

What AlphaSense Needs  ·  What I Bring

AlphaSense's two-part mission for this role is highly specific. Both halves map to work I have already shipped — not conceptually, but in production systems with measurable outcomes.

The Challenge
01
Measure the True Impact of GenAI Features on Retention

AlphaSense wants to understand whether new GenAI capabilities are actually inflecting long-term user retention — not surface-level feature usage, but statistically validated causal impact on WAU/DAU and habit formation. Correlation does not answer this. Causal inference does.

02
Wire BigQuery to LLMs So PMs Can Query Data in Plain English

The goal is to eliminate the "request-and-wait" analytics bottleneck. Product Managers should be able to ask complex data questions in natural language and get accurate, audit-able answers from BigQuery — without waiting for an analyst or writing SQL.

03
Design a Semantic Layer That Makes Data "AI-Ready"

LLM-generated SQL is only as good as the schema it queries. If table names, column aliases, and metric definitions are inconsistent or undocumented, the AI returns garbage. Building an AI-ready semantic layer is the prerequisite for everything else.

04
Be the Strategic Co-Pilot for Product Leadership

The PM team needs an analyst who proactively surfaces trends, drop-offs, and opportunities across all product features — not someone who answers tickets. This requires deep product intuition, statistical rigor, and the ability to turn findings into executive-ready narratives.
