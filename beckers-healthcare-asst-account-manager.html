Becker's Healthcare is a fast-moving, high-cadence media and events organization where account managers are relationship builders and operations anchors rolled into one. Success here requires someone who can run high-volume outreach, keep proposals and CRM records spotless, coordinate across editorial and production teams, and turn post-campaign data into the next renewal conversation. That is exactly what I have been doing for five years.

Challenges I Solve
Challenge

Long-Standing Client Relationships at Risk Without Continuity

Enterprise clients who have trusted a media partner for years expect to feel remembered, understood, and prioritized -- not handed off to someone starting from scratch every quarter.

My Response

Structured Check-Ins and Personalized CRM-Driven Outreach

Managed 12+ enterprise accounts at Infosys as primary relationship owner -- conducting regular check-ins, tracking engagement history in Salesforce CRM, and building outreach cadences tailored to each account's priorities and renewal timeline.

Challenge

Proposals and Collateral Take Too Long to Turn Around

A sales team moving fast can't wait three days for a custom proposal or media kit. Delays in collateral cost deals and signal to clients that the team isn't organized.

My Response

Templated Proposal Frameworks + 50% Faster Turnaround

Built modular proposal templates and custom one-sheet frameworks at Infosys, enabling account teams to personalize materials quickly for each client. Reduced proposal turnaround time by 50% and freed senior reps to focus on relationship conversations rather than document production.

Challenge

CRM Data Goes Stale -- Forecasting Becomes Guesswork

When account activity isn't logged consistently, pipeline reports mislead leadership and missed renewal dates slip through the cracks. Discipline in CRM hygiene is a competitive advantage.

My Response

Automated Pipeline Tracking + Real-Time CRM Dashboards

Maintained meticulous Salesforce records across 12+ accounts and built automated reporting workflows that reduced manual CRM data entry by 65%. Leadership always had real-time visibility into pipeline health, renewal timelines, and outreach activity without chasing account managers for updates.

Challenge

Post-Campaign Data Sits in a Spreadsheet No One Acts On

Campaign wrap-ups are only valuable if they translate into a retention conversation, an upsell, or a smarter next campaign. Most post-campaign summaries get filed and forgotten.

My Response

Post-Campaign Summaries Engineered as Renewal Conversations

Developed post-campaign performance reports at Infosys that synthesized engagement metrics into client-ready narratives -- framed not as "here's what happened" but as "here's what we do next." These summaries directly supported upsell conversations and influenced 10% YoY growth across the account portfolio.
