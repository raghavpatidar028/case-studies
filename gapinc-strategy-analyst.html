Gap Inc's Post Purchase team sits at the intersection of customer experience and operational strategy, where the difference between a loyal repeat buyer and a lost customer is often decided by how fast, clearly, and accurately the analytics team can turn contact center data into strategic direction. That translation -- from raw signal to executive action -- is exactly what I've been doing for five years.

Challenges I Solve
Challenge

Ambiguous Problems, No Clear Starting Point

Post Purchase strategy often begins with a vague directive -- "Why is CSAT dropping?" or "Where are we losing customers?" -- with no pre-packaged framework and imperfect data.

My Response

Structured Problem Decomposition to Workplans

At Infosys, I routinely translated ambiguous business questions into structured workplans using SQL and Python analysis -- identifying the key question beneath the surface, scoping the data needed, and delivering a defensible point of view with clear executive recommendations.

Challenge

Leadership Walks into Meetings Unprepared

Post Purchase leaders need to walk into stakeholder reviews fully armed -- with the right narrative, the right numbers, and a clear story that connects data to decisions. That requires an analyst who can synthesize, not just report.

My Response

Executive Narratives from Complex Data

I presented quarterly operational performance insights directly to C-suite, translating complex, dispersed data into clean storylines with supporting visuals. My dashboards and analyses informed budget reallocation decisions driving 10% YoY impact -- I don't just hand over charts, I hand over conclusions.

Challenge

Business Partners Waiting on Analytics for Every Question

When every data question requires a ticket to the analytics team, strategy slows to a crawl and trust erodes. Self-serve access only works when the models and definitions underneath are reliable and consistent.

My Response

Scalable Power BI Models + Databricks Data Products

Built reusable Power BI data models connected to Databricks-backed data products, giving business partners reliable self-serve access to operational metrics. Shared data model standards reduced ad-hoc requests by 22% and built cross-functional trust in the numbers.

Challenge

Issues Surface Too Late -- After They've Already Escalated

By the time a Post Purchase problem appears in a weekly report, it may already be a customer-facing incident. The value is in catching signals early, not documenting them after the fact.

My Response

Proactive Signal Surfacing with Anomaly Detection

Engineered demand forecasting models at 95% accuracy and automated anomaly detection pipelines that flagged issues before escalation. Consistently identified operational signals early, translating them into proactive strategy adjustments rather than reactive firefighting.
